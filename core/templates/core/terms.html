ForensiQ Terms of Use & Forensic Standard

Rules of engagement, evidentiary integrity obligations, and authorized usage standards governing internal auditors and forensic investigators operating the ForensiQ workstation.

Version 2.4 Effective: October 2026 Binding on all Authorized Investigators
Sections
1. Enterprise Mandate 2. Investigator Conduct 3. Credential Security 4. Non-Spoliation Rules 5. Export Restrictions 6. Auditor Verification 7. Disclaimers & Law
1

Authorized Enterprise Mandate & Permitted Use

Access to the ForensiQ Workstation is strictly restricted to designated internal auditors, corporate fraud examiners, and authorized legal investigators of Hyundai Motor India Ltd (HMIL).

The workstation must only be utilized for legitimate business inquiries, internal control audits, compliance reviews, and formal investigative proceedings authorized by the Audit Committee or Corporate Leadership.

Prohibited Use: Unauthorized personal snooping, interception of non-relevant private employee communications, or unauthorized disclosure of confidential investigation materials is strictly prohibited and constitutes a grave disciplinary offense.
2

Investigator Standards of Professional Conduct

All users must execute their investigative responsibilities with objectivity, professional skepticism, and rigorous adherence to evidentiary standards:

  • Fairness & Objectivity: Findings must be grounded in verified transactional records, forensically sound timestamps, and authenticated documents.
  • Strict Need-to-Know: Forensic dossiers, entity correlation graphs, and flagged transaction ledgers must only be disclosed to members of the investigative committee.
3

Authentication & Session Security

Users are individually responsible for safeguarding workstation access:

Session Locking

Investigators must utilize the top navigation Logout / Lock control whenever leaving the terminal unattended to prevent unauthorized workstation access.

No Key Sharing

Portal access keys and environment credentials must never be shared across unverified communications or stored in plain-text external repositories.

4

Non-Spoliation Rules & Anti-Tamper Mandate

Evidence tampering, retroactive manipulation of parsed outputs, or deliberate falsification of risk metrics violates forensic integrity guidelines and applicable law:

  • No modification of ingested raw artifacts (PST, CSV, PDF, audio files).
  • No bypass of cryptographic SHA-256 verification or database timestamps.
  • All exclusions or manual risk overrides must include a verifiable audit rationale.
5

Evidence Export & Dissemination Controls

Exported dossiers (Excel reports, Sankey diagrams, Tabulator dumps, and metadata inspection sheets) remain proprietary and confidential HMIL work-product. Exported files must only be saved to encrypted, authorized enterprise storage volumes.

6

Algorithmic Risk Scores & Auditor Verification

ForensiQ analytical modules (such as Q-Bank rapid pass-through scoring, Q-Verify document authenticity scoring, and Q-Mail anomaly checkpoints) provide automated triage assistance.

Human Auditor Verification Required: Automated risk indicators and fuzzy matching scores serve as decision support tools. Formal evidentiary conclusions and disciplinary referrals must be independently corroborated and verified by a qualified human forensic auditor.
7

Governing Law, Disclaimers & Compliance

Operation of the platform is subject to the Information Technology Act, 2000 (and subsequent amendments), Indian Evidence Act / Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam standards for digital records, and the internal governance charters of Hyundai Motor India Ltd.
