ForensiQ Privacy Policy & Evidence Governance

Standardized data governance guidelines, cryptographic chain of custody procedures, and privacy protections governing digital forensic artifacts within the ForensiQ analytical workstation.

Version 2.4 Effective: October 2026 Internal Audit & Assurance • Strictly Confidential
Sections

1

Scope & Investigative Jurisdiction

This Privacy Policy governs all evidence collection, analysis, storage, and cross-source correlation performed within the ForensiQ Workstation on behalf of Hyundai Motor India Ltd (HMIL) Internal Audit and Corporate Vigilance divisions.

ForensiQ operates exclusively in service of legitimate internal audits, fraud examinations, whistleblowing triage, and statutory regulatory inquiries authorized under the corporate audit charter.

Notice of Authority: All ingested records, digital custodians, and extracted metrics are processed under enterprise supervisory mandate for fraud prevention and internal controls verification.
2

Ingested Forensic Evidence & Artifacts

The platform processes forensic artifacts across multiple operational analytical modules:

  • Financial Transactions (Q-Bank & Q-Trail): Ingested bank statement PDFs/CSVs, account numbers, RTGS/NEFT/UPI transfer trails, and counterparty entities.
  • Enterprise Communications (Q-Mail & Q-Chat): Custodian PST archives, communication headers, message contents, metadata timestamps, and conversation threads.
  • Endpoint & File Systems (Q-Scan & Q-Verify): Filesystem hashes, author signatures, PDF structure trees, exif metadata, and matched keyword contexts.
  • Procurement & ERP Records (Q-Ledger): SAP/Oracle purchase orders, GRN records, invoice line items, and vendor master records.
3

Cryptographic Chain of Custody & Integrity

Evidence integrity is central to the legal defensibility of forensic audit findings:

SHA-256 Hashing

Every raw evidence file, extracted PST attachment, and uploaded statement is computed with a cryptographic SHA-256 digest upon intake.

Immutable Audit Logs

Every case creation, profile linkage, and search query is registered with high-precision timestamps and investigator session markers.

4

Data Retention, Preservation & Secure Disposal

Forensic files are retained strictly in accordance with statutory corporate compliance standards:

Active Case Preservation: Evidence tied to active audits remains locked and protected against alteration until formal conclusion of the investigation dossier.
Disposal Protocol: Upon expiration of required statutory limitation periods or explicit closure by the Audit Committee, digital files are purged using secure, non-recoverable overwriting procedures.
5

Access Controls & Zero External Egress

ForensiQ operates on an on-premise, zero-trust network topology:

  • No Cloud Egress: Ingested files and extracted datasets are never transferred to external cloud APIs or unauthorized third parties.
  • Master Portal Key: All interactive sessions require cryptographic credential verification via the secure authentication gateway.
  • Role-Restricted Workspaces: Evidence profiles are isolated per active audit to prevent cross-matter data contamination.
6

Whistleblower Safeguards & Privacy Protections

In accordance with the HMIL Whistleblower Policy and applicable statutory privacy mandates, informant identities and inadvertent bystander personal data (such as non-relevant family or medical communications in PST archives) are protected through strict relevancy filters and investigative redaction tools.
